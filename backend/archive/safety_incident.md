# Safety Incident Policy

## 1. Definition and Scope

A safety incident is an allegation of harassment, threats, unsafe conduct, assault, discrimination or another safety risk. Typical complaint: "Driver behaved inappropriately during the trip."
Category: safety_incident. This category is a triage and review workflow. It is not an autonomous guilt determination.
No financial outcome is decided automatically in this category.

## 2. Decision Variables

There are no monetary thresholds. The variables are classifications:

- Immediate or ongoing danger: the allegation describes a current or continuing threat.
- Non-immediate serious misconduct: a serious allegation with no current danger.
- Insufficient evidence: the evidence does not support a factual determination.
- Minimum record: incident description, trip reference, incident time, and a contact or safety flag.

## 3. Evidence Required

- Required: incident description, trip reference, incident time, contact or safety flag.
- Optional corroboration: relevant chat, route and stops, witness details, authorised incident attachments, live trip tracking or call-masking records where available.
- Protection: limit exposure of sensitive narratives. Do not request unnecessary personal details.
- Urgency: immediate or ongoing danger must be routed to emergency and safety support procedures.

## 4. Conduct Reference Points

These are reference points for a neutral evidence summary. They are not findings.

- Ryde's Driver-Partner Handbook prohibits comments on a passenger's appearance, or remarks on race, religion or other personal beliefs.
- It prohibits contacting a passenger after the trip has ended.
- It states that crimes are not tolerated on the platform.
- It states that drivers are given the opportunity to respond before final decisions, except in urgent or high-risk situations.

## 5. Decision Rules

- SI-001A: the allegation indicates immediate or ongoing danger. Outcome: urgent HUMAN_REVIEW and safety escalation, with emergency guidance where appropriate.
- SI-001B: non-immediate serious misconduct allegation. Outcome: HUMAN_REVIEW with a neutral evidence summary.
- SI-001C: insufficient evidence for a factual determination. Outcome: preserve the allegation and the uncertainty, and HUMAN_REVIEW.

## 6. Prohibited Automated Outcomes

- Definitive findings of criminal wrongdoing.
- Punitive account actions such as suspension.
- Dismissal of a serious safety allegation solely on model confidence.

## 7. Escalation and Fairness

- Every safety allegation is escalated under [general_principles#4], rule G-003.
- Present both parties' accounts in neutral language.
- Preserve the original allegation and any uncertainty in the record.
