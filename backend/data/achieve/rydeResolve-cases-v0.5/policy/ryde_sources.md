# Ryde published rules used as sources (read 8 and 12 Oct 2026)

All pages are on help.rydesharing.com/hc/en-us/articles/. Page text was read in full on 8 Oct 2026 unless noted.
Short tags are used in the policy file (S1, S2 and so on).

| S1 | 23534478435353 Cancellation and Waiting Fee (rider-facing) | On-demand: cancel within 3 min of matching is free. Cancellation fee S$6.61 if the rider cancels after 3 min of matching, OR the driver presses "I'm Here", waits more than 3 min for the rider to arrive at the pin, and cancels (rider no-show). Waiting time fee S$5 at the end of the trip if the driver pressed "I'm Here", waited more than 3 min for the rider to board and completed the trip. Advance trip: cancel more than 15 min before scheduled time is free; if the booking is accepted only after that point the rider gets a 3 min grace. ## Contradictions inside Ryde's own pages (used on purpose as test cases)

1. Waiting fee amount: S$5 (S1, S7), S$4.50 (S2, driver side), S$6.61 (S3).
2. Cancellation fee: S$6.61 charged to the rider (S1, S6) against S$4.50 received by the driver (S2). The gap is consistent with fees on top of the driver share (S1 footnote) but no page states the split.
3. Advance trips: S1 says the rider is charged if the driver waits more than 3 min and cancels. S2 lists that same driver-waits case under a waiting time fee of S$4.50, not a cancellation fee.
4. Cleaning caps: S19 and S20 cap bodily fluids (including pet) at S$120 and vomit at S$200. S21 says pet mess up to S$150 and vomit up to $140. The policy follows the claims table in S19 and S20 (PD-8).
