- Type: Property damage / cleaning fee; filed by the rider
- Complaint: "I was charged $35 for cleaning. The container only leaked a little."
- Key facts: claim food_drink $35.00, status charged, charged $35.00; submitted 4.0 h after the trip; 1 photo(s) showing food_drink; receipt: yes, $35.00
- Rider record: 0 past disputes (0 upheld), 0 fraud flags
- Driver record: 0 past disputes, 0 fraud flags, 0 warnings in last 30 days
- **Ruling:** charge_upheld; refund to rider $0.00; payer: n/a; driver warning: no; escalate: no
- Policy rules: PD-1, PD-3, PD-4
- Reasoning: Valid photos show spilled curry and rice and the car's plate; complete receipt from a registered cleaner for S$35, under the S$40 food and drink cap. The S$35 fee stands (PD-3, PD-4).

### PREC-PD-02 - Urine claimed as vomit
- Type: Property damage / cleaning fee; filed by the rider
- Complaint: "My son had an accident in the car and I accept a cleaning fee, but $180 seems too much."
- Key facts: claim vomit $180.00, status charged, charged $180.00; submitted 10.0 h after the trip; 1 photo(s) showing bodily_fluids; receipt: yes, $180.00
- Rider record: 0 past disputes (0 upheld), 0 fraud flags
- Driver record: 0 past disputes, 0 fraud flags, 0 warnings in last 30 days
- **Ruling:** partial_refund; refund to rider $60.00; payer: driver; driver warning: no; escalate: no
- Policy rules: PD-4, PD-8, G-6
- Reasoning: Claimed as vomit (cap S$200) but the photos show urine, which is bodily fluids (cap S$120). The S$180 receipt is capped at S$120, so S$60 of the S$180 charged is refunded. The driver's claim caused the overcharge, so the driver pays (PD-4, PD-8).
