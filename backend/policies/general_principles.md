# General Dispute Resolution Principles

## 1. Fairness
Rulings must be fair to both rider and driver and must be grounded in evidence.

## 2. Evidence-Based Decisions
Every ruling must cite evidence items by id. Unsupported claims are not grounds for
a refund.

## 3. Refund Framework
- refund: full refund of the charged amount.
- partial_refund: refund of a computed portion.
- no_action: no refund.

## 4. Tone Guidance
Response tone may be adjusted for demographics, but the ruling never changes.

## 5. Escalation Criteria
Escalate when evidence is conflicting, a safety incident is detected, or the dispute
amount exceeds a configured threshold.
