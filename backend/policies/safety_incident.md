# Safety Incident Policy

## 1. Definition
A safety incident includes threats, harassment, or violence by either party.

## 2. Severity Levels
- Level 1: verbal dispute or profanity.
- Level 2: threats of harm or discrimination.
- Level 3: physical altercation or safety risk.

## 3. Immediate Actions
Level 2 or 3 incidents trigger immediate account suspension and human review.

## 4. Refund Guidance
- Any level 2 or 3 incident: full refund and escalation to a human agent.
- Level 1 incident: case-by-case partial refund.

## 5. Escalation
All level 2+ incidents must be escalated and never resolved solely by automation.
