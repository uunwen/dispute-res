# Fare, Surge and Promo Policy

## 1. Fare Calculation
Fare = base fare + (per-km rate x distance) + (per-min rate x duration). The surge
multiplier is then applied and any promo discount is subtracted.

## 2. Surge Policy
The surge multiplier charged must match the multiplier quoted at booking. A surge
multiplier above 1.0 requires a documented surge event.

## 3. Promo Policy
Promo codes must be validated at booking. A valid promo that was not applied is a
fare error.

## 4. Refund Guidance
- Surge charged without a matching booking quote: refund the surge premium.
- Fare arithmetic error: refund the discrepancy.
- Valid promo not applied: refund the promo discount amount.

## 5. Thresholds
- Fare discrepancy tolerance: 0.02 currency units.
- Maximum allowed surge multiplier: 3.0.
