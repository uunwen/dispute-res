# No-Show and Cancellation Fee Policy

## 1. Definition
A no-show occurs when either party fails to appear for pickup within the defined
proximity and time thresholds.

## 2. Driver No-Show
A driver is a no-show when their GPS never reaches within 100 m of the pickup point.
The rider must not be charged a cancellation fee.

## 3. Rider No-Show
A rider is a no-show when the driver arrived within 100 m and waited for at least
5 minutes without the rider appearing. The cancellation fee is valid.

## 4. Cancellation Fee Thresholds
- Proximity threshold: 100 m.
- Minimum wait time: 5 minutes.
- Cancellation fee amount: configured by market (default 6.00).

## 5. Refund Guidance
- Driver no-show: full refund of the cancellation fee.
- Rider no-show: no refund; the fee is valid.
- Ambiguous (driver arrived but waited under 5 min): refund the fee.
